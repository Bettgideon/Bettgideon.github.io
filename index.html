About Me

Full-Stack Web Developer, Data Analyst & ICT Specialist

Hi, I'm Gideon Kiprotich Bett, a Full-Stack Web Developer, Data Analyst, and ICT Professional based in Nairobi, Kenya. I hold a BSc. in ICT Management (Second Class Honours, Upper Division) from Maseno University. I design and build modern, responsive, and scalable web applications that solve real-world problems, and I turn data into clear, useful insights.

I currently work as a Junior Data Analyst at Angata Sugar Mills (since October 2026), where I work with data, databases, and reporting using SQL, MySQL, Excel, and Power BI. Before that, I gained hands-on experience as an IT Support Intern at NHIF, handling technical support, Microsoft 365, network administration, and ICT asset management.

My technical skill set includes PHP, JavaScript, React.js, Vue.js, HTML5, CSS3, MySQL, API Integration, UI/UX Design, and Database Management. I write clean, efficient, and secure code and follow industry best practices. I also hold Cisco CCNA, Cybersecurity Essentials, and CyberOps Associate certifications, which support my work in networking and security.

Through GManTech Solutions Ltd, I have worked on projects across sectors such as agriculture, education, business management, e-commerce, and digital services. Notable projects include KEFARM, a Smart Agriculture and Food Security platform that uses technology to improve agricultural productivity and sustainability in Kenya, as well as an Emergency Response System and a Hotel Booking System.

I am passionate about continuous learning, data analytics, emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and software innovation. My goal is to build impactful digital products that meet business objectives and create real value for communities and organizations. Whether collaborating with a team or working independently, I approach every project with dedication, creativity, and a commitment to excellence.
